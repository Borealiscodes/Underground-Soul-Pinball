## ⭐ Bill Nye Says…

> “Kids!  
> 
> Have you ever noticed how a movie or video game can make you feel nostalgic just by playing **six familiar notes** on a music box?!  
> 
> Composers call that a **leitmotif**—a short musical theme tied to a character or feeling! When *Undertale* creator Toby Fox built his game largely solo while dealing with painful wrist injuries, he turned limitation into superpower: by taking one simple melody and changing its **speed, rhythm, and softness**, the same six notes can sound like a cozy waltz by a fireplace, a lantern walk in the snow, or a grand finale!”

## ❤️ Part 1: Three Ideas Behind This Tribute

1. **One Leitmotif, Four Realms (`gentle-musicbox-audio.js`):**  
   All four tracks in this tribute transform the same 6-note theme (`F4 – C5 – B♭4 – A4 – G4 – F4`) through a warm low-pass filter (`580 Hz – 860 Hz`) so the music stays gentle and unhurried.
2. **Pacifist Pinball Mechanics (`ACT → MERCY → SPARE`):**  
   Instead of destroying targets, every `[A]`, `[C]`, and `[T]` drop target and Waterfall Orbit loop raises the **Mercy Meter** toward `100%`—turning the central guardian golden so you can **SPARE** them and advance to the next realm.
3. **True Skill-Based Pinball Geometry (`36 px` Gap):**  
   With a `36-pixel` center gap for an `18-pixel` crimson SOUL orb, return inlanes, risky outlanes, and a `12-second` Ball Save, every flip is a genuine test of timing and determination!
